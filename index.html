Ground Rules
Movement snack runs alongside everything. It does not compete with heavy days.
Half snatch form first. Rest longer before chasing reps with a broken pattern.
ABC press ladder: quality over quantity. Stop when the press slows, not when you hit a number.
Jefferson curl: start at 8-12kg. Build over weeks, not sessions.
Lower back routine daily. 10 minutes every day beats 60 minutes once a week.
Alternate Ab Routine A and B on Tue/Thu. Together they cover every core pattern.
CARs are not warm-up swings. Slow enough to own every degree of range.
